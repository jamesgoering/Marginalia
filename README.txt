MARGINALIA FOR iPAD AND PHONE  -  version 0.5 (demo data)
=========================================================

WHAT TO UPLOAD
Upload everything in this folder to your hosting site, keeping the
folders as they are:

  index.html              the app
  manifest.webmanifest    name and icons for "Add to Home Screen"
  sw.js                   lets the app work offline
  fonts/                  the app's fonts (no outside font service)
  icons/                  Home Screen and browser icons
  README.txt              this file (optional to upload)

It can live at the top of your site (example.com/) or in its own
folder (example.com/marginalia/). All links inside are relative.

The site must use HTTPS (an address starting https://). Offline use
and installing to the Home Screen only work over HTTPS.

INSTALL ON iPAD OR iPHONE
1. Open the app's address in Safari.
2. Tap the Share button, then "Add to Home Screen", then Add.
3. Open Marginalia from the Home Screen icon from now on.
After the first visit it works with no internet connection.

YOUR NOTES
- Marks, notes, text settings and devotional entries are saved on
  that device only. They do not sync yet.
- The Home Screen app keeps its own copy, separate from Safari. Use
  the Home Screen app consistently.
- Back up from the info (i) button > "Back up my notes". "Restore a
  backup" brings a backup file back.

UPDATING LATER
Upload the new files over the old ones, and make sure the VERSION
line at the top of sw.js has changed. The next time the app is
opened online it shows "A new version is ready - Reload".
